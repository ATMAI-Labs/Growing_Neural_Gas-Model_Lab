Does prior A training help when A returns?

Two training histories receive the same intervening B stream and identical return-to-A samples. The canonical algorithm is unchanged. This experiment measures the effect of prior geometric experience.

Interpretation boundary

  • A lower return error supports an effect of prior training history under this protocol. It does not establish semantic identity, explicit concept matching or a new memory mechanism.
  • Representation error and topographic error answer different questions. Both remain visible even when their changes disagree.
  • Recovery bands are operational tolerances fixed from independent pre-change evaluation, not confidence bands. Stationary behavior shows their calibration limits.
  • The bootstrap resamples seed-level paired differences. Its interval is descriptive; there is no aggregate pass or multiple-comparison-adjusted superiority claim.
