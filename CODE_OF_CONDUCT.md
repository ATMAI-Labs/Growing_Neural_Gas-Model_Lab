# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-oriented research
community. Participants should:

- critique claims, methods, and evidence without attacking people;
- welcome questions and clearly distinguish uncertainty from error;
- credit contributions and preserve provenance;
- disclose conflicts, limitations, and negative results;
- protect personal, clinical, confidential, and security-sensitive information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized conduct, and
  deliberate disruption.

## Scientific conduct

Fabricated results, undisclosed copied code, false authorship, manipulated
evidence, or knowingly unsupported capability claims are unacceptable.
Good-faith mistakes are handled through correction and learning.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an
appropriate private GitHub channel. For security or sensitive-data concerns,
use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently remove access when necessary. Maintainers will
avoid public disclosure of a reporter's identity or sensitive details except
where required for safety or law.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.
