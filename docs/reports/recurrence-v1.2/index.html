01 / EXPERIMENT DESIGN

Same budget.
Different histories.

Each pair receives the same intervening B stream and identical return-A samples. Warmup lasts 10,000 updates, with a common 100-node cap and matching live-node counts at every scored step.

Warmup distribution is the treatment. Lifetime B exposure differs: 4,096 samples for prior A, 14,096 for B-only history. Edge counts and geometry may still differ.
